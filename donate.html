Institutional Stewardship

Help Build the Foundation

Support can help ALIH develop the foundation needed for responsible, community-led work.

Resource Allocation

Where Your Support Contributes

Depending on the organisation's needs and verified funding arrangements, support may contribute to:

01

Community Assessment & Engagement

Direct household consultations, village listening sessions, and community prioritization dialogues across Hoima District.

02

Research & Learning

Data collection, mapping of existing social services, identifying genuine gaps, and publishing open findings.

03

Organisational Development

Building reliable institutional systems, financial accountability software, safeguarding compliance, and staff training.

04

Education Initiatives

Strengthening local learning environments, supporting inclusive basic education, and foundational literacy/numeracy.

05

Child Protection

Safeguarding systems, girl-child retention support, safety protocols, and dignity initiatives for vulnerable young people.

06

Youth Opportunities

Connecting young women and men with practical skills, vocational mentorship, and sustainable economic pathways.

07

Family & Community Resilience

Strengthening household capacities, community support networks, and sustainable livelihood resilience.

08

Future Programmes

Carefully designed future initiatives strictly founded on demonstrated community need, feasibility, and sustainability.

Our Stewardship Commitment

We are committed to responsible use of resources and to maintaining accountability to the people and partners who support our work.

To discuss supporting ALIH, please contact us.

Whether you represent a foundation, an institution, or are an individual supporter, our executive leadership welcomes direct dialogue.
