Working With Others

ALIH does not intend to work alone. Our aim is to connect existing strengths and opportunities, identify gaps, and develop partnerships where collaboration creates greater value.

Our Partnership Tenet
«We do not want to duplicate what is already working. We want to understand it, strengthen it where appropriate and contribute where a genuine gap exists.»

We operate with deep humility toward local actors who have labored for years in Western Uganda. We seek alignment, transparency, and mutual accountability.

Collaborative Network

Who We Build Relationships With

Sustainable community transformation requires multi-sectoral coordination. ALIH actively partners across eleven key spheres:

Communities & Families

Direct household listening & community ownership

Schools & Educators

Primary, secondary, and host-settlement institutions

Local Civic Leaders

Village elders, subcounty councils, and cultural authorities

Government Institutions

District local governments and ministerial departments

Community Organisations

Grassroots women groups, youth associations, and CBOs

Civil Society (CSOs)

National and regional advocacy and welfare organizations

Development Partners

Multilateral and international humanitarian entities

Foundations

Philanthropic foundations supporting long-term systems

Private-Sector Partners

Enterprises committed to regional economic inclusion

Technical Organisations

Institutions providing curriculum, vocational & legal expertise

Skilled Individuals & Knowledge Contributors

Researchers, educators, technologists, and advisors
