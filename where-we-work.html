Where We Work

Hoima District, Western Uganda

Our Base

Rooted in Hoima District

ALIH is based in Hoima District, Western Uganda.

Our work begins with understanding the communities around us and the wider local environment in which people live, learn, work and seek opportunities.

As the organisation develops, any expansion will be guided by evidence, capacity, partnerships and responsible planning.

Western Uganda is a region rich in cultural heritage, agrarian vitality, and entrepreneurial youth. However, many rural households face persistent hurdles in accessing high-quality schooling, child protection mechanisms, and structured pathways into modern vocational livelihood opportunities.

Institutional Stage

Our Current Stage: Foundation & Community Assessment

ALIH is currently laying its foundation. We believe it is better to build carefully than to grow quickly without strong foundations.

PRIORITY 01

Listening

Understanding community experiences, household aspirations, and local priorities directly from community members.

PRIORITY 02

Assessment

Gathering information about needs, opportunities, existing community services, and local initiatives.

PRIORITY 03

Relationships

Building durable trust with communities, educational institutions, government stakeholders, and potential partners.

PRIORITY 04

Learning

Using evidence and direct field experience to guide future decisions and programme designs responsibly.

PRIORITY 05

Institution Building

Developing the systems, governance procedures, safeguarding mechanisms, and operational structures needed for responsible long-term work.

“We believe it is better to build carefully than to grow quickly without strong foundations.”
