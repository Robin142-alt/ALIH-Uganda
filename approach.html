We Listen Before We Act

ALIH believes that good interventions begin with deep understanding. Rather than deploying assumptions, we systematically evaluate what already exists to determine where we can contribute something truly distinctive.

« Evidence before expansion. » — The governing discipline across every ALIH initiative
Methodology Standard

What We Rigorously Seek to Understand Before Intervening:

Before designing or implementing any project in Kyangwali Subcounty or Kikuube District, our team addresses eight diagnostic questions:

01 What already exists in the community
02 What is already working effectively
03 What organisations and institutions are doing
04 What services are available to families
05 Where the real systemic gaps are located
06 Who may be currently underserved or left behind
07 What communities themselves identify as priorities
08 Where ALIH can contribute something useful and different

Sequential Process
PHASE 01 — METHODOLOGY STAGE

01. LISTEN: Community and household consultations

We sit down directly with community members, elders, teachers, parents, and youth in their own environments. We never approach a community with pre-fabricated solutions or assumed needs.

Core Operational Activities
  • Household-level listening sessions across villages
  • Focus groups with parents, youth, and local leaders
  • Documenting lived challenges and community-defined priorities
  • Ensuring inclusive participation without bias or rush
