Foundation Phase Hoima District Assessment

Commencement of Initial Community Assessment Framework

ALIH has initiated preliminary community assessment protocols across target rural clusters in Hoima District. The assessment prioritizes household-level dialogues with mothers, youth, village elders, and local leaders. In alignment with our creed—We listen first—the goal is to map existing local capacities, document real barriers to education, and assess gaps before considering any programmatic deployment.

Governance Institutional Structuring

Leadership Structuring & Core Stewardship Principles Established

Under the leadership of Executive Director Muhumuza Jackson, Program Director Alinaitwe Christopher, and Chief Financial Officer Aikiriza Christine, ALIH has codified its 10 governing values and foundational policies. This includes embedding our child safeguarding charter and financial accountability mechanisms into all operational planning from day one.

Partnership Dialogue Ecosystem Engagement

Open Consultation Invitation for Researchers, Educators & Civil Society

In upholding our principle to Build on What Exists and avoid duplicate systems, ALIH is opening exploratory dialogues with academic researchers, local educational institutions, and community-based organisations in Western Uganda. We invite individuals and entities with technical expertise, research experience, and volunteer capacity to connect with our secretariat.

Verified updates will be published here periodically as our field learning and institutional milestones progress.
