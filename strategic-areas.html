Strategic Direction

Our Focus Areas

Our long-term direction is developing around six connected areas.

These are ALIH's areas of focus and long-term direction. They should not be understood as all being fully operational programmes at this stage.
AREA 01

Education Access & Quality

We aim to support children and communities to access meaningful, inclusive and quality education.

Focusing on community learning spaces, foundational literacy, numeracy, and strengthening existing primary school environments in Hoima District.

AREA 02

Girls' Education & Child Protection

We aim to contribute to girls' education while promoting children's safety, dignity, rights and protection.

Working closely with families, elders, and schools to safeguard vulnerable children, reduce dropouts, and create protective, encouraging community environments.

AREA 03

Youth Skills & Opportunity

We aim to connect young people with practical skills, information, mentorship and pathways to opportunity.

Bridging the gap between raw potential and sustainable livelihoods through technical vocational mentorship, life skills, and civic responsibility.

AREA 04

Family Resilience & Livelihoods

We aim to support families to strengthen their abilities, resilience and access to sustainable opportunities.

Because children thrive when households are stable, we explore household economic resilience, climate-smart agriculture, and local savings systems.

AREA 05

Community Opportunity Hub

We aim to connect people with useful information, services, skills, resources, relationships and opportunities.

A physical and relational junction where community members access advisory guidance, collaborative spaces, and connection to broader networks.

AREA 06

Future Education Development

Where evidence and community priorities support the case, ALIH may explore future education initiatives.

Any future institution or education project will depend on community need, feasibility, sustainability, partnerships and responsible planning.

Interested in Collaborating on a Focus Area?

Whether through technical advisory, evidence generation, or institutional partnership, we welcome conversation.
