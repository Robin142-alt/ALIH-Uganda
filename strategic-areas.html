What We Work Around

ALIH's long-term work is organised around six strategic areas. Specific programmes will be developed based on evidence, community priorities, available opportunities, and partnerships.

Distinguishing Institutional Principle

« ALIH will not automatically establish a school whenever an education need is identified. »

This principle is central to our integrity. Rather than arriving with predetermined construction projects, we evaluate the ecosystem using a disciplined three-stage decision sequence:

Stage 01

What is already available?

We catalog existing public, private, community, and settlement schools. We evaluate physical capacity, teacher ratios, learning materials, and accessibility.

Stage 02

Where is the gap?

Is the friction point classroom seating, teacher retention, girl child safety and sanitation, vocational training relevance, or household poverty?

Stage 03

What produces the greatest sustainable benefit?

Does systemic benefit come from upgrading an existing facility, providing child protection support, or developing a specialized future learning hub?
