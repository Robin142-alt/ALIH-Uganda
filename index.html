Who We Are

Building from the Community

AbilityLink Impact Hub (ALIH) is a Ugandan Community-Based Organisation based in Hoima District, Western Uganda.

We are building ALIH carefully, starting by listening, learning and understanding the communities we hope to serve. We want to build an organisation that works with people, strengthens what already exists and creates opportunities that can have lasting value.

Learn More About ALIH
Our Current Stage

Foundation & Community Assessment

ALIH is currently building its foundation and learning from the communities we hope to serve.

We are gathering information, understanding what already exists and identifying where there are genuine opportunities to contribute.

“Good work starts with good understanding.”

Working With Others

We Believe in Partnership

Good community development does not happen alone.

We want to work with communities, schools, local leaders, government, researchers, organisations, volunteers and other partners who can bring knowledge, experience, resources or opportunities.

Partnership with ALIH is about more than fundraising. It is about learning, contributing and building together.

Our Commitment

Building Something That Can Last

We want to build ALIH beyond short-term projects and individual people.

This question guides how we think about community ownership, partnerships, accountability, learning and institutional development.
