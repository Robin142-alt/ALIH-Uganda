Governance & Stewardship

How Our Leadership Governs

Our directors hold themselves to rigorous standards of transparency, integrity, and servant leadership.

PILLAR 01

Integrity & Truthfulness

We aim to be honest in what we say and do. We share what we are actually doing, not creating stories simply to look successful.

PILLAR 02

Community Ownership

We believe lasting solutions should involve the people they are intended to serve. Leadership listens before setting direction.

PILLAR 03

Safeguarding Priority

The safety, dignity and wellbeing of children, young people and vulnerable individuals are foundational to every programmatic decision.
