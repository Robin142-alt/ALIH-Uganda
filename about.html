Institutional Overview

Who We Are

AbilityLink Impact Hub (ALIH) is a Community-Based Organisation in Uganda, working from Hoima District, Western Uganda.

Our Purpose

Connecting Ability to Opportunity

Our purpose is to connect people and communities with knowledge, skills, resources and opportunities that can contribute to better lives and stronger communities.

We are starting carefully, learning from the community and building the systems, relationships and partnerships needed for responsible long-term work.

At ALIH, we want to move beyond short-term thinking and build approaches that can create lasting value for communities. We believe that true, lasting community development begins by understanding the realities of children, youth, and families in Hoima District, identifying existing strengths, and collaborating to build durable institutions.

Our Vision

Our Mission

«To connect children, families and communities with education, protection, skills, resources and opportunities through inclusive programmes and partnerships.»
Our Story

Where the Idea Comes From

ALIH grew from a simple belief:

People have abilities. Communities have potential. The right connections can create opportunity.

The organisation was created with the intention of building something that can grow beyond one project, one donor or one individual.

Our goal is to build strong foundations, learn from experience and develop an institution that can continue creating value for communities over time.

Our Values

Our Values

Ten values that guide how we work, make decisions and build relationships with communities and partners.

01

Integrity

We aim to be honest in what we say and do.

02

Human Dignity

We respect every person's worth.

03

Inclusion

We believe people should have the opportunity to participate.

04

We take responsibility for our decisions and resources.

05

06

Safeguarding

We take the safety and dignity of children and vulnerable people seriously.

07
