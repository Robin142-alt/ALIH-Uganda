Data Stewardship

Privacy Policy

ALIH respects the privacy of people who communicate with us.

We aim to collect only information that is necessary for communication, programme engagement, partnership discussions or other legitimate organisational purposes.

1. Responsible Data Handling

Information provided to ALIH will be handled responsibly and will not be unnecessarily shared with others.

We treat communications from community members, local partners, donors, researchers, and volunteers with confidentiality and care. We never sell, lease, commercialize, or arbitrarily distribute contact details or private messages.

2. Website Data Minimization Principle

The website should collect only the information required for its forms and communication functions.

  • Contact Inquiries: Name, email address, optional telephone number, and message content submitted through our contact form are used exclusively to respond to your inquiry.
  • Partnership Submissions: Organisation name, collaboration track, and relevant discussion points submitted through our intake form are used strictly to explore institutional cooperation.
  • No Intrusive Tracking: We do not deploy invasive third-party ad trackers, tracking pixels, or monetized user profiling tools.

3. Community Assessment Data Protection

When conducting community assessments in Hoima District and Western Uganda, all household dialogues, focus group responses, and qualitative notes are collected under strict informed consent. Data is aggregated and anonymized to protect individual family identities, safeguarding vulnerable persons and respecting community dignity.

4. Inquiries & Privacy Contact

If you have any questions regarding how your communication or information is handled by AbilityLink Impact Hub, please contact our secretariat:
